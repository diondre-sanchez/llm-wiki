# Real vs. Perfect Love

## Definition

Real love is defined as an attainable, grounding connection that sees, connects with, and validates the inherent goodness of a person. It is explicitly contrasted with perfect love, which is described as an unattainable ideal. Real love is characterized as "light, not heavy," serving to remind individuals that they are "never asking for too much" [[A Gentle Reminder]].

## How it works

The distinction operates by shifting the standard of romantic success from an idealized, flawless state to a realistic, reciprocal presence. Real love functions by:

*   **Validation:** It confirms that a person's depth of feeling is not a burden but a worthy trait.
*   **Safety:** It allows individuals to lower their walls and share "hidden parts of the soul" without fear of being vilified for their "darkened past" or healing methods [[A Gentle Reminder]].
*   **Reciprocity:** It involves a partner who chooses the individual deeply and fights for the relationship, rather than requiring the individual to "quiet the way they care" [[A Gentle Reminder]].

This concept rejects the notion that love must be perfect to be valid. Instead, it posits that the "right person" holds one's love without requiring suppression of one's nature [[A Gentle Reminder]].

## Examples

The source distinguishes real love from other emotional categories:

*   **Hurricane Love:** Described as filling hearts with doubt, dishonesty, and questioning of worth. This is the antithesis of real love, which provides stability rather than chaos [[A Gentle Reminder]].
*   **Sunrise Love:** Characterized by tenderness, peace, and softness, leaving the individual kinder to themselves. While positive, real love is the broader framework that encompasses this softness without the intensity of a "hurricane" [[A Gentle Reminder]].
*   **Perfect Love:** An unattainable standard that, when pursued, leads to the belief that one is "too much" or that one must beg for love. Real love is the realistic alternative that affirms worthiness without perfection [[A Gentle Reminder]].
