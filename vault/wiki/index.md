## Concepts

- [[Ingest]] - The core operation of reading a new source, writing its summary, and updating all relevant wiki pages to compound knowledge.
- [[Lint]] - A periodic wiki health-check operation that identifies contradictions, stale claims, orphan pages, and missing cross-references to maintain knowledge base integrity.
- [[LLM Wiki]] - A persistent, LLM-maintained knowledge base that compounds over time instead of re-deriving answers via RAG.
- [[Memex]] - Vannevar Bush's 1945 vision of a personal, associative knowledge store, realized by LLM-maintained wikis.
- [[RAG]] - Retrieval-Augmented Generation is the query-time retrieval baseline that the LLM Wiki pattern contrasts against by compiling knowledge into a persistent, compounding wiki.
- [[Schema]] - The configuration document that defines wiki structure, conventions, and workflows, turning a generic LLM into a disciplined wiki maintainer.
