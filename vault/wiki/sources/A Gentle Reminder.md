# A Gentle Reminder

## What it is

*A Gentle Reminder*, dedicated "for the deep feelers," is a 2020 self-help book by [[Bianca Sparacino]], published by [[Thought Catalog Books]]. The book addresses individuals who feel they are "too much" in their emotional intensity, arguing that their depth is a strength rather than a flaw. It serves as a guide for healing, setting relationship standards, and finding self-worth independent of external validation.

## Key claims

- **The Right Person:** The correct partner chooses you deeply without requiring you to suppress your nature. You do not need to beg for love or quiet your care; the right people will stay and fight for you.
- **Self-Closure:** Healing is an internal act. Waiting for external closure places power in another person's hands. The book asserts that "no closure is closure" and that individuals must grant themselves permission to let chapters end unmended.
- **Real vs. Perfect Love:** Perfect love is unattainable. Real love is defined by seeing you, connecting, and validating that you were "never asking for too much." It is light, not heavy, and does not flinch at a "darkened past."
- **Vulnerability as Courage:** Closing off to avoid hurt also closes off awe and joy. The book urges readers to "unhinge your rib cage" and share their hearts, arguing that bravery is the quiet act of showing up imperfectly and tenderly.
- **Solitude as Foundation:** Being alone is not synonymous with weakness. It is a "gift" and a "steady ground" that allows one to be their own home. This self-reliance prevents "bankrupting" one's identity to keep "half-loves" in life.
- **Energy Transformation:** Citing the scientific law that energy cannot be created or destroyed, the book reframes grief. The love and energy of lost people are not gone but transformed into the world, meaning "we never really lose the people we lose."

## Data and quotes

- **Biological Resilience:** The book cites that taking one step forward utilizes **200 million cells** and that skin sheds every **27 days**, symbolizing the physical letting go of past burdens.
