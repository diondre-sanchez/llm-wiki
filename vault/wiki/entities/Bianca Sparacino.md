# Bianca Sparacino

## Overview
Bianca Sparacino is a writer from Toronto known for her work on love, healing, and self-worth. She is the author of *A Gentle Reminder* (also referred to as *For the Deep Feelers* in some contexts), published in 2020 by [[Thought Catalog Books]]. Her writing focuses on validating deep emotional experiences, encouraging vulnerability, and redefining standards for romantic and personal relationships.

## Key Facts
- **Location:** Toronto, Canada.
- **Primary Role:** Writer and author.
- **Notable Work:** *A Gentle Reminder* (2020), published by [[Thought Catalog Books]].
- **Related Works:** *The Strength In Our Scars* and *Seeds Planted In Concrete*.
- **Online Presence:** Active on social media under the handle "rainbowsalt" (Instagram and Facebook).

## Relationships
- **Publisher:** Works with [[Thought Catalog Books]], an imprint of The Thought & Expression Company LLC.
- **Themes:** Her work explores concepts such as [[Vulnerability as Courage]], [[Real vs. Perfect Love]], and [[Solitude as Foundation]].
