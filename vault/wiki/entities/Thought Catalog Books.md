# Thought Catalog Books

## Overview
Thought Catalog Books is a publishing imprint owned and operated by The Thought & Expression Company LLC, an independent media organization based in Brooklyn, New York, and Los Angeles, California. It functions as the book division of the digital magazine Thought Catalog, which was founded in 2010 [[A Gentle Reminder]].

## Key Facts
- **Corporate Structure:** Thought Catalog Books is an imprint of Thought Catalog, which is owned by The Thought & Expression Company LLC [[A Gentle Reminder]].
- **Founding:** The parent organization, Thought Catalog, was founded in 2010 [[A Gentle Reminder]].
- **Mission:** The organization aims to help people become better communicators and listeners, and to help creatives realize their artistic vision in print and digital formats [[A Gentle Reminder]].
- **Locations:** Operations are based in Brooklyn, New York, and Los Angeles, California [[A Gentle Reminder]].
- **Related Entities:** The Thought & Expression Company LLC also owns Shop Catalog (a curated shopping platform) and Collective World (a creative community network) [[A Gentle Reminder]].

## Relationships
- **Parent Company:** The Thought & Expression Company LLC [[A Gentle Reminder]].
- **Sister Imprints/Platforms:** Shop Catalog and Collective World [[A Gentle Reminder]].
- **Authors:** Publishes works by authors such as [[Bianca Sparacino]], including *A Gentle Reminder*, *The Strength In Our Scars*, and *Seeds Planted In Concrete* [[A Gentle Reminder]].
