# Vannevar Bush

## Overview

Vannevar Bush was an American engineer and scientist who, in 1945, proposed the concept of the Memex — a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents. He is cited as the intellectual ancestor of the LLM Wiki pattern, whose design is described as being "related in spirit" to his vision [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].

## Key facts

- Proposed the Memex concept in 1945 [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].
- The Memex was envisioned as a private, actively curated knowledge store where the connections between documents were as valuable as the documents themselves [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].
- Bush's vision is characterized as being closer to the LLM Wiki pattern than to what the web became [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].
- The unresolved problem in Bush's design was who performs the maintenance of the knowledge store; the LLM Wiki pattern addresses this by delegating maintenance to an LLM [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].

## Relationships

- **[[Memex]]** — The concept Bush originated; the direct intellectual predecessor of the LLM Wiki pattern.
- **[[LLM Wiki Pattern]]** — The modern pattern that cites Bush's Memex as its spiritual ancestor and solves the maintenance problem he could not.
